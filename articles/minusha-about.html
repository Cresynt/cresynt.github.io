A Little Background

Hello! I'm Minusha, or you could call me Minnie. I'm a queer artist and a graphics designer from Sri Lanka. I've also had an interest in writing and game development. Originally starting from Scratch... I- I mean the Visual programming language Scratch, if you can even call it that, I have slowly built up my skills through years of just messing around. You can find some of my work over on my website.

Just like Saad, I've always wanted to make an indie game. I have started multiple projects alone, such as "Traceback", "You're just Paranoid" and "Terminus". While I always enjoyed working on the visuals, it's another story when it comes to actually coding. I do know multiple programming languages, including python, and the whole suite of languages you need for web development. But... It's just too much work for me.

That's why I and Saad decided to make a game studio together! Splitting our work between even just two people, means twice as less work for one. Let me introduce you to arrOw_LEft, originally known as Cresynt studios.
